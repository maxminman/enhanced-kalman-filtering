Quick verdict

✅ Plan 2 contains the right ingredients: ensemble filters (EKF/UKF/PF/IMM), ultra-high fidelity forces (EGM2008, NRLMSISE-00), SRP+albedo, JPL ephemerides, space-weather inputs, adaptive measurement handling, batch multi-arc fits, and divergence handling.

⚠️ But the devil is in the details: parameter estimation (ballistic coeff / SRP scale), measurement modeling (TLE→measurement noise), and validation rigor are the factors that make or break consistent sub-1 km performance when you only use public TLEs for observations.

🧪 Practical outcome: With this plan implemented and well-tuned, you can reach sub-1 km for the majority of LEOs. For a few corner cases (very low altitudes, highly tumbling objects, or rapidly decaying debris) there will be residual difficulty without real sensor data — but even those will be much better than raw SGP4.

Top risks & why they matter

TLEs are only noisy, intermittent measurements — they limit achievable absolute accuracy. Your algorithms can compensate but cannot magically create external truth.

Atmospheric density uncertainty (space weather) drives most of LEO error — NRLMSISE + real-time F10.7/Kp helps, but models are still uncertain.

Model complexity vs stability — EGM2008 to 70×70 and full albedo + thermal terms are computationally expensive; they may introduce numerical stiffness and overfitting unless you regularize parameter estimation.

Parameter observability — CdA and CR are only weakly observable from TLE updates alone; you must use sliding-window batch estimation + adaptive priors + occasional regularization (or particles) to get robust convergence.

Validation pitfall — comparing at times not in OEM, or interpolating inappropriately, can give misleading results.

What to prioritize (must-do, order matters)

TLE measurement model & R(age)

Build a robust empirical mapping from TLE age → measurement covariance using historical TLE→OEM residuals. This single step massively improves filter weighting and stability.

NRLMSISE + space weather pipeline

Automate ingestion of F10.7/Kp (or proxies if you can’t fetch real-time) and feed those to the density model. Make density model configurable for tuning.

Augmented-state EKF + sliding-window batch fit

EKF with CdA/Cr as slow states, plus a periodic batch solve (Gauss–Newton/LM) over many TLE epochs to robustly estimate CdA. The batch fit fixes the slow / weak observability problem.

Fixed-lag smoother (RTS)

Apply smoothing over the recent window (300–600 s). This converts filtered estimates to smoother ones and reduces per-step error visible at OEM timestamps.

Adaptive Q/R and innovation monitoring

Implement innovation-based covariance matching, automatic covariance inflation, and divergence detection/auto-reinit to avoid filter pathologies.

Ensemble fallback (UKF/PF/IMM)

Use EKF as the main workhorse; run UKF/PF in parallel only when EKF indicates high nonlinearity or parameter instability — this keeps compute reasonable while giving robustness.

Residual-corrector ML model

Train a small LSTM/conv model on EKF→OEM residuals to remove remaining structured bias. This is the final 100s→10s-of-meters improvement often required to pass sub-1 km consistently.

Extensive validation harness

Strictly evaluate only at OEM timestamps (or using high-quality interpolation), run 48–72h validation for each test satellite, report RMS, P95, %<1km. Do ablation tests (with/without each module).

Tuning & observability notes (practical scientific details)

Fit CdA over a sliding window of many TLEs spanning multiple orbits — this increases observability vs trying to learn it from a single update. Use Levenberg-Marquardt with bounds (physical priors).

Use a small along-track empirical acceleration term in the state (slowly varying) — it captures unmodeled atmospheric and maneuver-like biases.

For low altitude (<350 km) objects, increase process noise and shorten batch windows; their density variation is higher and less predictable.

Regularize high-degree gravity terms: don’t enable 70×70 unless you've tested numerical stability and have strong reason (EGM2008 high-degree mainly affects very precise applications; J2..J4/J6 often suffice for many LEOs).

Validation acceptance criteria (how you’ll know it’s real)
